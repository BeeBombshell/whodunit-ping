Whodunit is a small, self-hosted form. A visitor writes a message, your phone buzzes. No emails, accounts, cookies or IP addresses are collected.

Private by design, not by promise.

A visitor writes

Your form takes text and nothing else. No login, no email field, no cookie.

Your server forwards it

The message goes to your ntfy topic. The sender’s address is dropped before anything is written.

Your phone buzzes

You read the message. There is no sender to look up, so there is nothing to leak.

Self-host in minutes.

Run with Docker 
docker run -d -p 3000:3000 \
  -e NTFY_TOPIC=my-secret-ping-topic \
  ghcr.io/beebombshell/whodunit:latest

Or deploy with Cloudflare Workers (npx wrangler deploy) in 60 seconds.

Embed <whodunit-form> 
<script type="module" src="https://unpkg.com/@whodunit/widget"></script>
<whodunit-form endpoint="https://ping.example.com/ping"></whodunit-form>

Works on Hugo, Astro, Next.js, Ghost, or vanilla HTML.
